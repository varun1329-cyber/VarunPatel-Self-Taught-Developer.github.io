02 A little about me

Self-taught.
Still curious.

I’m Varun Patel, a solo learner exploring coding through practical projects. My focus is building iOS apps, and Xcode is where I bring those ideas together.

I use AI tools as learning partners: ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Perplexity, and Claude. They help me ask questions, explore approaches, and work through concepts I’m still learning.

My aim is to move beyond getting code to work and toward understanding why it works. That takes practice, patience, and a willingness to start again when something doesn’t make sense.

This website is a small record of that journey. No expert claims—just curiosity, ongoing projects, and steady progress.

03 How I approach it

  1. 01

    Begin with something small.

    A clear idea. One screen. One useful feature. Something manageable to build, explore, and understand.

  2. 02

    Use AI. Stay curious.

    My goal is to ask better questions, examine the explanations, and understand the code rather than simply collect it.

  3. 03

    Try, test, and refine.

    Run the app, notice what doesn’t work, and improve it a little at a time. Each iteration is another chance to learn.
